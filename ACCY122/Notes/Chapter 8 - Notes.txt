Bank Recon and Petty Cash

Bank Recon 

Bank reconciliation basically means
- bank bal != book
- so we need to adjust until they both balance 

Step 1: Adjust the bank side 1st

  Outstanding checks
  (Checks issued but not yet processed/cleared by bank)
  Subtract from bank statement
- cuz already recorded in books, Bank has not recorded yet

  Deposits in transit
  (Deposits recorded in books but not yet processed by bank)
  Add to bank statement
- cuz already recorded in books, Bank has not recorded yet


Step 2: Adjust the book side

- bank svc fee = subtract from book

- interest earned = add in the book 

- NSF cheque (customer bounced cheque) = subtract from book 

- Check #841 was correctly processed by the bank as $1,962.54 but was mistakenly recorded
  by you as $1,562.54 
  - if u issue a check means cash go down
    - if bank > book = subtract the difference from book (UNDERSTATED)
    - if bank < book = add the difference in the book (OVERSTATED)




Petty Cash

Petty cash is a current asset as it is cash the company keeps on hand for small payments 
(like stamps, taxi fares, office snacks, etc.)

So when fund is created:
DR Petty cash (cash on hand) increases 
	CR Cash at bank decreases



What is cash shortage?

When spend money from petty cash fund 
- after accounting all the receipts and it shd have this amt remaining
- but a lesser amount is found
- that cash shortage could be 
  - lost money
  - wrong change given
  - no receipt given for that shortage amt
