Inventory

2 Types of Inventory System
- Periodic Inventory System
- Perpetual Inventory System 

Periodic Inventory System 
- Inventory is only updated at end of accounting period 
  - COGS is calculated at the end
  - COGS = Beginning Inventory + Purchases - Ending Inventory 

Typical Users: 
- dont sell high vol everyday
- dont need real-time stock tracking
- rely on physical stock counts at intervals (monthly, quarterly, yearly)
- Ex. Small retail shops, restuarants/cafes, hawkers

Limitations:
- U don't know each stock levels until u count everyth
- Harder to detect theft or shrinkage quickly 


Perpetual Inventory System
- Inventory is updated continuously after every purchase and sale 
  - COGS is recorded immediately after every purchase and sale 
  
Typical Users: 
- sell freq or in high vol
- need real-time stock tracking
- rely on tech system like POS, Scanners, ERP 
- Ex. Supermarkets, Uniqlo, Zara, Ecomm biz 

Limitations:
- More expensive to set up (software + systems)
- Requires accurate data entry systems



Inventory Valuation Method
- FIFO (First-In-First-Out)
- LIFO (Last-In-First-Out)
- WAVCO (Weight Average Cost)
- SIM (Standard Inventory Method)

Inventory valuation methods are used to determine value of inventory
and COGS in accounting and biz operation 
- higher COGS -> lower profit
- lower COGS -> higher profit
