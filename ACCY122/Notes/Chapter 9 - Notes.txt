Non Current Asset - Acquisition cost, depreciation

NCAs are assets used for long term 
- tangible NCAs = physical existence
  - land, vehicles, equipment
- intangible NCAs = no physical existence
  - patents, trademarks, goodwill 

Acquisition Cost

AC = Purchase price + all incidental expenses (transport + installation + test run)

Depreciation

Before calculating depreciation, 2 estimates are required:
1. Useful (economic) life
2. Residual/resale/disposal/scrap value 

Ex. 

Machinery total cost = 43k
Residual value = 3k
Total depreciable amount = 43k - 3k = 40k
Useful life = 4 years 


To calculate Depreciation, we use 4 common methods
- Straight Line Method 
- Reducing Balance Method (dont need deduct from residual value)
- Sum of Digits Method
- Output/Unit of Production Method


1. Straight Line Method (Furniture, buildings, phones)

Annual dep = (Total Cost − Residual Value) ÷ Useful Life

Ex. 

(43k - 3k) / 4 = 10k/year
 
*prorate: If bought on 1 Oct Year N, Year 1 gets only 3/12:
- 10k × 3/12 = 2.5k



2. Reducing Balance Method (Equipment, vehicles)

A constant % is applied to the carrying amount each year

Ex.

Dep rate is 48.5%

Year 1: 43k - (43k x 48.5%) = 22.145k
Year 2: 22.145k - (22.145k x 48.5%) = 11.405k
Year 3: 11.405k - (11.405k x 48.5%) = 5.873k
Year 4: (5.873k - (5.873k x 48.5%)) + 25 = 3k

*$25 adjustment in Year 4 ensures amount is exactly at the estimated residual value



3. Sum of Digits Method

Logic: Proportionate, front-loaded. Digits are summed (4+3+2+1 = 10) and used as weights.
Suits: Assets with decreasing usefulness over time

Ex.

43k - 3k = 40k 

year 1 rate is 4/10 so 40% of 40k = 16k (dep)
year 2 rate is 1/10 so 10% of 40k = 10k (dep)
...


4. Output/Unit of Production Method (Mining assets, oil reserves)

depreciate based on actual usage not time

Ex.

total units = 10k hours

(43k - 3k) / 10k = 4/hour 

each unit = 4

year 1 = 2200 hours = 4 x 2200 = 8800 (dep)
year 2 = 2460 hours = 4 x 2460 = 9840 (dep)
...


Accounting Entry for Depreciation

Each year in journal:
DR Depreciation Expense (income statement)
	CR Accumulated Depreciation (bal sheet) *accum dep is contra asset account dr - / cr +


In bal sheet:
Equipment (at cost)              43k
Less: Accumulated Depreciation   20.855k
Carrying Amount / Book Value     22.145k


Disposal of NCA
1. Sold at book value = no gain/loss
2. discarded the NCA = loss of 3k
3. sold above book value (4k) = gain of 1k 
4. sold below book value (1.5k) = loss of 1.5k
