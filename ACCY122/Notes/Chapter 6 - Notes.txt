Types of Journals and ICS

5 types of journal

Sales journal - Records credit sales only
Purchase Journal - Records credit purchases only
Cash Receipt Journal - Records all cash coming IN
Cash Payment Journal - Records all cash going OUT
General Journal - Records everything else



Internal Control Systems

A system of internal control consists of all the measures used by a business:
• to safeguard its resources against waste, fraud and inefficiency
• to promote the reliability of accounting data
• to encourage compliance with business policies and government regulations


2 types of internal control
- Administrative
- Accounting

Administrative Control
- Focus on how the business is run efficiently and whether employees follow company policies

Ex.
- Hiring procedures (e.g. approval before hiring staff)
- Sales and purchasing procedures
- Performance reports (to monitor employee work)

Accounting controls
- Focus on protecting money and ensuring financial records are correct.

Ex.
- Only authorised people can approve payments
- Different people handle cash and record transactions
- Checks to prevent errors in accounting records

Principles of a good internal control system
• Clearly established lines of responsibility.
• Separation of record keeping and custodianship.
• Division of responsibility for related transactions.
• Mechanical and electronic devices.
• Adequate insurance.
• Internal auditing.
• Programming, physical and other controls.

Limitations of internal control systems
- Good controls can break down due to tiredness, indifference or carelessness
- Heavy reliance on segregation of duties may lead to collusion


Advantages of Computerized accounting
- Speed in processing
- Error reduction
- Faster response time
- Automatic posting 

Disadvantages of Computerized accounting
- Virus
- Hackers
- Fraud
- Power failure (system down)
