Bad debts

A biz sells goods on credit 
- Customer buys now and pay later

But sometimes customers never pay
- That unpaid amount = Bad Debt

Ex.

Company sells $200 on credit:

DR Account Receivable 200
	CR Revenue 200

Customer disappears or goes bankrupt
The $200 becomes bad debt

Using direct write off method:

DR Bad debt expense 200
	CR Accounts Receivable 200



The direct write off method is weak cuz
1. matching concept
   - expenses shd be recorded in the SAME period as the revenue
   - ex. revenue recorded in 2024, bad debt exp recorded in 2025 

2. prudence concept
   - accounting shd anticipate likely losses early 


Allowance method 
- considers matching concept and prudence concept
- instead of waiting for bad debts to occur, we estimate bad debts earlier


Ex.

Company sells $200 on credit:

DR Account Receivable 200
	CR Revenue 200

We think some debts may become bad 

Allowance method:
- suppose estimated bad debt = 125

DR Bad debt expense 125
	CR Allowance for Doubtful Debts 125

Allowance for DD is a contra asset acc so dr - / cr +

Net AR = AR - Allowance (we expect to only collect net account receivable)


Customer officially defaulted

Dr Allowance for Doubtful Debts     125
    Cr Accounts Receivable               125

- reduce the allowance (doubtful debts)
- reduce accounts receivable (asset)


How to calculate estimated allowance 

1. income statement method
   - credit sales x %

Ex. 

Based on past experience, 3% of credit sales may become bad debts.

200k × 3% = 6000

Dr Bad Debt Expense      6000
    Cr Allowance for DD      6000


2. bal sheet method
   - use agewise analysis = older debts have higher chance of default

Ex.

| Category   | Amount | % Uncollectible |
| ---------- | ------ | --------------- |
| Not due    | 12,000 | 1%              |
| 1–15 days  | 9,000  | 3%              |
| 16–30 days | 27,000 | 10%             |
| 31–60 days | 11,000 | 20%             |
| 61–90 days | 10,000 | 40%             |
| Over 90    | 6,000  | 70%             |

Add everyth up (credit sales x %)
120 + 270 + 2700 + 2200 + 4000 + 4200 = 13490 

Required allowance = 13490
Existing allowance = 4000
To increase allowance by 13490 - 4000 = 9490

Dr Bad Debt Expense           9,490
    Cr Allowance for Doubtful Debts     9,490
