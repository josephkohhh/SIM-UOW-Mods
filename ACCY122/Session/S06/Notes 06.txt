internal control systems will be in final exams

internal control systems consists of all measures used by a biz to
- safeguard its resources against, fraud, waste and inefficiency
- ensure reliability of accounting data
- encourage compliance with biz policy and govt regulation

2 TYPES OF ICS
- administrative
- accounting

administrative control
- focus on operation of biz and human resource follow biz policy
  - performance report
  - hiring procedure
  
accounting 
- focus on protecting money ensuring financial records are correct
  - diff ppl handle cash and record trans


good principles of ICS
- internal auditing
- clear and defined lines of responsibility
- adequate insurance


limitations of ICS
- heavy reliance of segregation of duties can lead to collusion
- ICS may break down due to carelessness, tiredness or indifference

Advantages of Computerized accounting
- Speed in processing
- Error reduction
- Automatic posting 

Disadvantages of Computerized accounting
- Virus
- Hackers
- Fraud
- Power failure (system down)
