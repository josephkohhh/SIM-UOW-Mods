Gibbs Reflective Cycle

Reflection = Thinking about experience to learn (learning through experience)

it helps with:
– Better decision-making
– Better leadership

6 Stages of Gibbs’ Cycle
- Description
- Feelings
- Evaluation
- Analysis
- Action Plan
- Conclusion


Description (What happened?)
- What the situation was
- Who was involved
- What you did (briefly)
- What the outcome was


Feelings (What were you thinking and feeling?)
- How you felt before, during, after
- Why you felt that way
- Show how your feelings changed over time 
- Link these changes to any issues or developments within the group 


Evaluation (What worked well or didn’t?)
- What went well
- What didn’t go well
- Be balanced (both positives and negatives)

 Analysis (Why did things happen that way?)
    •	Select ONE specific challenge, conflict, or success from your experience 
    •	Apply ONE relevant management theory (choose from): 
        - Team management (Week 3) 
        - Motivation (Week 3) 
        - Leadership (Week 4) 
        - Conflict (Week 5) 
        - Power and politics (Week 6) 
    •	Analyse the situation using the chosen theory   
    •	Explain your role in influencing the team’s outcome  


Action Plan (What will you do in future?)
•	Outline specific steps to improve based on your analysis 
•	Ensure steps are: 
    - Clear 
    - Realistic 
    - Achievable 
•	Link your plan to the chosen theory 
•	Present your plan using SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)

    
Conclusion (What did you learn?) 
- Summarise key takeaways 
- What you learned from the experience
- What you would do differently (briefly, not detailed yet)
