These are the CSCI322 Final Exam format and sample questions.

The exam has 3 parts:
Section	Format	Marks
Part A	20 MCQs	20
Part B	10 short-answer questions	20
Part C	2 planning and design questions	10
Total		50

What this tells us about exam preparation
1. Part A – MCQ: Understand key concepts, terminology, differences and purposes.
2. Part B – Short answer: Be able to explain concepts clearly and write basic Linux commands.
3. Part C – Planning and design: Apply your knowledge to practical Linux server scenarios. Explain your design choices and justify them.


Q & A
• Do I need to remember all commands?
– No, but you need to remember some most basic ones,
e.g., ping www.google.com
• Do I need to remember all the files and their formats?
– No, but you need to remember some most basic ones,
e.g., the password file
