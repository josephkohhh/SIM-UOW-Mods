What is a sys admin?
A person who manages 1 or more computer systems, often working alongside database, network, 
security and web administrators and computer operators. 


7 goals of a sys admin:

1. Reliability
- Percentage of time a system or service is usable. Linked to SLAs.
- Measured by uptime and monitoring tools (Nagios, Cacti, SNMP)
- 99.99% (4 9s) = 52min downtime per yr
- 99.999% (5 9s) = 5min downtime per yr

2. Integrity
- Data can be trusted not to be lost or damaged
- Affected by storage design, backup procedures and protocol/hardware design
- Tools: Bacula, Amanda, Tripwire

3. Security
- Data is not destroyed, altered or disclosed to unauthorised parties.
- Tools: Snort (IDS), Nessus (vulnerability scanning)
- Tools are useless without procedure and policy. High security usually means lower usability.

4. Performance
- Systems should run as fast and efficiently as possible. 
- Influenced by hardware, OS, software bugs, tuning and user practices. 
- Don't assume "a bigger box" fixes everything

5. Efficiency
- Maximise the productivity of admin staff.
- Often conflicts with other goals: 
  - centralising eases admin but may hurt performance
  - automating accounts may hurt security

6. Ease of use
- Users can do tasks the way they want. 
- Conflicts with security, performance and integrity. 

7. Order
- Compliance with laws, regulations and policies:
  - copyright law
  - ethics
  - equal opportunity
  - harassment policies
