Scenario: Online University Course Registration System
A university wants to develop an Online Course Registration System to replace its existing manual registration process.

Currently, students must visit the administration office to register for courses. This process is slow and often results in errors, such as students registering for courses they are not eligible to take.

The new system should provide the following features:
- Students can log in and browse available courses.
- Students can register for courses if they meet the prerequisites.
- Students can drop courses before the registration deadline.
- Administrators can create, update and remove courses.
- Administrators can view student registration records.
- The system should prevent students from registering for courses that have reached maximum capacity.
The university has also stated that requirements may change during development because different departments may request additional features.
