Biz Logic:

Insurance policy
Each policy contains:
- Policy number
- Coverage type
  - Third Party Only (Damage/injury to other party)
  - Third Party, Fire & Theft (Third party + fire + theft)
  - Comprehensive (Virtually all costs involving the insured car)
- Coverage value
- Inclusions
- Exceptions
- Risk level
- Premium
- Excess


What is excess?
It is the amt of money the policyholder has to pay themselves when making a claim

Every claim has a basic excess

Ex. 
Basic excess = $500
Your approved claim = $5,000

The insurance company doesn't pay the full $5,000.
$5,000 − $500 excess = $4,500 payout

In this case, 
Excess increases when:
- Driver is under 25
- Driver has held licence for less than 1 year

If claim is approved -> Excess is deducted from payout
If claim is rejected -> Policyholder receives a bill

***to be clear:
2 kinds of payment:
1. policyholder pays for the annual insurance policy 
2. policyholder also pay for the excess only if making a claim 



What is No-claim bonus?
It is a discount on your insurance premium for not making claims
- think of it as a reward for having a claim-free history

In this case, 
Policyholder who made no claims for at least 2 years:
- qualify for a no-claim bonus that gives 10% discount on the annual premium

But if after make a claim:
- disqualify for the no-claim bonus



What is Driver rating?
a score that represents the driver's claim/accident record and 
can affect how much premium they pay for insurance in the future

In this case,
- If the driver is at fault in an accident → driver rating decreases by 1.
- Also, the act of making a claim itself affects next year's insurance premium as well
