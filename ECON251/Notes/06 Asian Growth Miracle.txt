Growth Models & Strategies

Key characteristics for these asia economies

1. Industry-focused
Growth focus particularly on manufacturing. (from agriculture to industralization)

2. Outward-oriented
Economies use export promotion tools , both short-term and long-term
- Special Economic Zones (SEZs) = tax benefits and incentives 
- Trade Agreements = free Trade policy (low tariffs and barriers)
- Exchange-rate policies = weaker currency = making exports cheaper for foreign buyers 

3. High dependency on capital
Capital accumulation (K) plays an important role in increasing:
- Output
- Productivity

4. Government Intervention (Neoclassical approach)
- Mnimal government intervention

Asian Miracle = Manufacturing + Exports + Capital + Government policies

These economies have strong evidence of moving away from agriculture to industralization
Industrialisation does not necessarily mean industry remains dominant forever

The development path can be:
Agriculture → Manufacturing → Services


Flying Geese Theory
It explains how countries industrialise by following a more advanced country

Japan → Asian Tigers → ASEAN
- Japan = Lead Goose
- (Singapore, South Korea, Hong kong, Taiwan) Asian Tigers = Tier 2 Geese
- Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia = Tier 3 Geese


How Flying Geese works?

Step 1: Japan starts producing cheap goods
Japan initially has low labour costs, so it produces things like:
- Textiles
- Clothing
- Simple manufactured goods 

These are low-value-added goods

Step 2: Japan becomes too expensive for some production
Japan eventually becomes richer as it produces and exports more
- More production → Higher income → More savings → More investment
- As Japan becomes richer, Japanese workers' wages increase
- Japan becomes too expensive for some production
- So they invest in countries with cheaper labour which is called FDI 

Step 3: Production moves to other Asian countries
Production moves to other Asian countries
Japanese companies invest in countries such as South Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong and Singapore
- teach them Japanese production tech, management, methods, etc 

They then start producing the same types of low-value goods that Japan used to produce.

Step 4: The Asian Tigers eventually become expensive too
South Korea, Taiwan, Singapore and Hong Kong become richer
- Production then moves to Tier 3 Geeses (Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia)

The model is basically a chain reaction

Japan FDI + technology -> Asian Tigers FDI + technology -> ASEAN 


Taiwan & Korea = protection first, exports later.
